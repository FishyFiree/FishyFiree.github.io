Video Refiner

An AI video enhancer built with Python & PySide6 — frame interpolation (RIFE v4.26) to smooth motion and super-resolution (Real-ESRGAN) to sharpen detail. Input any source framerate/resolution and output a higher one (60→120fps, 1080p→4K, …), even non-integer multiples. Composable pipeline: interpolate first at native resolution, then upscale with aspect-ratio preservation, multi-model selection, and auto tiling for VRAM. Chinese desktop GUI with batch queue, side-by-side comparison playback, a CLI, and audio passthrough. NVIDIA GPU (CUDA) required.
